# Exact performance candidate: diagnostic pilot

The preselected 12-case panel completed 48/48 cells and 152 trials in [run 43614116593](https://github.com/guluchen/PathsumWMC/actions/runs/43614116593). This is a diagnostic panel, not a new whole-suite speed claim. The selection was committed before measurement in performance-pilot.json.

The candidate uses exact balanced input/output scheduling, immutable cached preparation and exact dyadic complex arithmetic with a general GMP-rational fallback. Functional source is d563342/670b985, frozen validated site is [43613464526](https://github.com/guluchen/PathsumWMC/actions/runs/43613464526). Validation passed 49 Rust tests, an independent state-vector gate/random ladder with a caught input-Y mutant, 26000 GMP-rational arithmetic comparisons, 96 real weighted-CNF browser oracles and 8 forced external distance cases, plus Stop/mobile tests. The candidate was never deployed to public Pages.

On the nine selected MQT Hybrid inputs, original default/proportional each returned seven numerical decisions, candidate native seven finite narrow intervals and candidate WASM five. Native had two timeouts; WASM had two timeouts and two errors. All three Feynman reduction cases now completed in both candidate builds, including hwb6 and qcla_adder10 that were previously incomplete in our broader sequential-schedule screen. There were no native/WASM interval conflicts, inconsistent repetitions or missing cells.

The selected same-host Hybrid request-time ratio medians versus original default were 3.49x native (six completed pairs) and 8.02x WASM (four pairs). Against Proportional they were 3.19x and 7.99x. These medians mix single screening trials and conditional five-repeat medians, omit unresolved cases rather than imputing them, and compare different numerical guarantees. They do not establish full-suite regression/improvement ratios against our previous runs on different CI hosts.

Some cases improved substantially, but the practical catch-up objective was not met. Grover5 candidate native took 14.085 s versus the same-host original's 0.257 s first trial; QFT16 took 5.365 s versus 1.299 s; QNN8 took 16.215 s versus 5.682 s and still exhausted browser memory. More exact-arithmetic/encoding optimization may be possible, but this round does not justify replacing the public default or claiming parity with QuPRS.

Per the PI's conditional instruction, work proceeds to a separately attributed [QuPRS browser fork](https://github.com/guluchen/QuPRS). The fork uses current upstream v0.16.0 Rust, not the historical Python reference of this panel. It must be validated and benchmarked separately; numerical results must not be labelled certified distance intervals. Exact candidate changes and all pilot data remain on this experimental branch.
